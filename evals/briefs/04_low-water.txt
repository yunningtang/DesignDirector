Design and build a complete, responsive official launch website for an original indie video game that is coming soon to PC and consoles.
Invent a compelling game concept, including its title, genre, fictional world, visual identity, and core gameplay features. The website should feel like a real promotional experience created by a professional game studio, rather than a generic landing page.
Include a cinematic hero section with the game title and release information, an interactive navigation system, a game overview, key gameplay features, a media gallery with screenshots and a trailer presentation, a story or world introduction, platform availability, and prominent wishlist or pre-order calls to action. Include a press kit or FAQ section.
Use the website's layout, typography, imagery, motion, transitions, and interactions to communicate the atmosphere and identity of the game. Consider how users discover content, move between sections, interact with media, and receive feedback from their actions.
Make the experience responsive across desktop and mobile devices. All navigation and interface controls should work, and any unavailable external services should be clearly represented through functional demo interactions rather than broken links.
You are free to determine the art direction, design language, animation style, page structure, and interaction patterns. Prioritize a distinctive, cohesive, engaging, and usable experience.
