Design and build an interactive web experience for exploring a fictional city's public transportation system. Generate a realistic sample dataset of transit routes, stations, ridership, and travel times. Include an interactive network visualization, at least two complementary data charts, filtering controls, station details, and coordinated highlighting across views. Users should be able to explore patterns, compare routes, and understand how selections affect the displayed information. Focus on legibility, meaningful interaction, accessible explanations, and clear feedback when filtering or selecting data.
