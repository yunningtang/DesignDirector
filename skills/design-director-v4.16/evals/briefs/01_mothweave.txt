Design and build a complete, original 2D browser game that can be played using a keyboard and mouse. The game should have a clear objective, intuitive controls, progressively increasing difficulty, a scoring system, and distinct start, pause, game-over, and restart states. Include a short interactive tutorial that introduces the mechanics through gameplay. Pay attention to moment-to-moment feedback, visual clarity, responsiveness, and the overall feel of the controls. The game must be fully playable without external accounts or services.
