# Craft floor (independent of style) <!-- rule:craft-budget -->

Follow silently while building.
- **Spacing:** one scale, base 4 (operate) or 8; only values from the scale. More space above a heading than below it.
- **Faces:** the assigned display face for headlines only; navigation, buttons, labels, tables and the wordmark use the body face. Do not swap in a serif or a novelty face for character. <!-- rule:real-type -->
- **Scale by mode:** operate, read and transact pages size type like real products (the script's numbers). Persuade and experience pages open big: the first-screen headline or image is the one dominant move (display about 2–3x the real median), then the page settles to product sizes.
- **Type:** one ratio (operate 1.2, read and transact 1.25, persuade and experience 1.333) with at least 3 clear levels. Body 16px+ (15px only in dense operate tables), line-height 1.5–1.7, measure 60–75ch. Use the display size the script prints; at most 8rem and at most 3 lines; tracking never tighter than −0.04em. `text-wrap: balance` on headings, `pretty` on body. Tabular figures for numbers that line up.
- **Colour:** only the palette.py variables. The page background is `--bg` (white, black or tinted, as drawn); `--surface` is only for cards, panels and inputs. Under committed or drenched colour the coloured area is frame-only: header, hero, bands, footer. <!-- rule:frame-ground --> Secondary text on a coloured area is tinted from that hue, never grey.
- **Corners:** exactly the two assigned values (containers, controls). Media (images, drawings, video) stay square unless containers are large: most real sites square their images even when their buttons are round. <!-- rule:media-square -->
- **Depth:** at most 2 elevation levels; every shadow has an offset and a blur. No hard offset shadow unless the direction is a printed or stamped world.
- **Motion:** one authored moment; the rest is feedback. 100–150ms feedback, 150–300ms state changes, 300–500ms overlays, at most 800ms for the one entrance. Animate from a visible state, never from invisible content. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **States:** hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, loading, empty and error for every interactive element. Theme the browser surfaces: focus ring, caret, selection, scrollbar.
- **Direction:** pull at least 3 concrete cues from the direction; on operate pages at least one cue does a job (marks state, groups, shows progress). <!-- rule:cues-do-jobs --> One dominant move; everything else quiet. <!-- rule:one-move -->
- **Real content shape** <!-- rule:real-content -->: sample content has the shape of real content: uneven lengths, a long name that wraps, a missing image, a zero, an item out of stock, a date in the past. Draw imagery from the content itself (the chart from the data, the book from its title and colours, the plan from the project) rather than adding decorative illustration.
- **Pictures with atmosphere** <!-- rule:atmospheric-imagery -->: on shops, bookings and stories about places, food, rooms, objects or trips, draw each item as a small scene (SVG or canvas) with a light source, a foreground and background, and 3+ colours from its setting; vary them per item. One-colour pictograms or the same icon on every card are a template tell.
- **Colour area** <!-- rule:colour-area -->: on operate, read and transact pages no single flat colour block takes more than about a third of the first screen; colour beyond that comes from content.
- **Truth:** sample content is labelled as sample; no invented logos, metrics, reviews or history. <!-- rule:no-invented-claims -->
- The second half of the page uses the same system as the first screen.


## Dragging never selects text (V4.8)
Any surface you drag on, such as a calendar grid, event blocks, a canvas, a board, a timeline, sliders or resize handles, gets `user-select: none` and `-webkit-user-select: none`. Its pointerdown handler calls `preventDefault()` and clears any selection with `getSelection().removeAllRanges()`. Opening a dialog or sheet also clears the selection. Otherwise a drag on a real trackpad paints the browser's selection colour over every label and placeholder on the page. Text inputs inside those surfaces keep normal selection.


## V4.13 rules (borrowed from the Taste and Impeccable skills, 2026-11-19)
- **Copy first** <!-- rule:copy-first -->: before any layout, write the first-screen sentence, every section heading and every button label into meta.json `copy`. A heading says what is in the section ("Rooms from ¥18,000", "This week's orders"), never a slot name ("Features", "How it works", "Overview"). Buttons say the action and its object ("Book this room", not "Get started"). No filler words (revolutionise, unlock, seamless, supercharge, elevate, effortless, empower). check.py lists slot-name headings and filler.
- **Sample label** <!-- rule:sample-label -->: invented figures (dashboards, stats, prices in a demo, league tables, network scores) carry a quiet "Sample data" tag next to the heading of the block that shows them, once per block. check.py flags pages with many bare figures and no such label.
- **First-screen task** <!-- rule:first-screen-task --> (operate, read and flow pages; premium pages see rule:premium below): at 1440×900 and at 375×812 the control that starts the primary task is on the first screen, and on the phone at least half of the working area (the form, the board, the canvas, the result) shows before any scroll. Run check.py with `--primary "<css>" --core "<css>"` naming those two elements. On the phone, put the working area before intro text, not after it.
- **Button family** <!-- rule:button-family -->: one primary, one secondary and one quiet button style for the whole page, plus icon buttons. Tabs, chips and toggles are not buttons. check.py counts the looks.
- **Focus and tap** <!-- rule:focus-visible -->: every control shows a visible focus ring when reached with Tab (outline or ring in the accent, 2px, offset 2px); on the phone, buttons and fields are at least 44px tall. The page does not scroll sideways at 320px wide either (check.py tests 320px).
- **Photo library first** <!-- rule:photo-library -->: before searching the web, open the photo library index (`scripts/photos.py`, library in `data/photolib2/`, built by `scripts/fetch_photos.py`), filter by topic, view a contact sheet of the candidates with Read, and use the ones that fit (credits are in the index). Search Wikimedia live only for a subject the library lacks.
- **Real photographs** <!-- rule:real-photos -->: on shops, bookings, venues, travel, food, furniture, fashion, real estate, architecture, museums, film and photography (the PICTURES line), you may use real licensed photographs instead of drawn scenes. Search Wikimedia Commons (its API at https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/api.php with `generator=search&gsrnamespace=6&prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|extmetadata&iiurlwidth=960` (Wikimedia serves only standard thumbnail widths: 250, 330, 500, 960, 1280, 1920; any other width is refused with HTTP 429)), download candidates into your work folder, make one contact sheet (PIL) and look at it with Read, and keep photos that show the subject without readable brand names, logos or a person's face as the subject. Keep the 960px thumbnail as it comes (do not request other widths), JPEG quality about 70, and embed as data URIs (the page stays one file; keep all photos together under about 2 MB). Credit each photo (author, licence) in one footer line. Open each saved file with PIL before embedding; check.py lists images that fail to decode (rule:photo-decode). Photos are stand-ins: say so in meta.json. Send a descriptive User-Agent header (for example `DesignBenchmark/1.0 (bench@example.org)`); on HTTP 429 wait 30 seconds and retry, at most three times, then fall back to drawn scenes. Never hotlink. Drawn scenes stay the default for operate pages and wherever a photo would be generic.


## V4.14 small-object rules (from the task 12 firing-stamp page, 2026-11-19)
- **Object host** <!-- rule:object-host -->: the object sits where it really exists on its component: a tag at the picture's edge, a label line under the name, the base of a vessel, a line on the receipt. It never floats over the product picture and never covers the product.
- **Quiet when repeated** <!-- rule:object-quiet -->: on cards that repeat, the object is ink or a neutral or the item's own colour, small (a strip or a corner, at most about a twelfth of the card), and never the accent: the accent belongs to the primary action. It may be full-size and coloured in one place only, the detail view or the selected state.
- **Decision facts** <!-- rule:object-decides -->: it shows what a buyer compares (material, size, origin, condition, time), ideally the same values the filters use, so the object is also the filter. Never a stock or urgency count.
- **Legible**: text on the object is 12px or larger and runs straight; no text on a circular path, no micro-type.


## V4.15 shop colour (2026-11-19)
- **Shop colour place** <!-- rule:shop-colour-place -->: real shop and booking homepages open on pictures, not on a flat colour band (2 of 74 reference homepages had one). On transact pages there is no full-width flat colour band that holds only a headline and a button; colour lives in the pictures, the primary button, the selected filter, category tiles or a filter rail, and at most one slim promo line. The primary button's accent is not also the colour of a large block.


## V4.16 premium tier (2026-11-21)
Applies to persuade, experience and shop/booking landing pages (assign.py prints NEXT 3c PREMIUM for them). Adapted from Hallmark (MIT) layout.md, imagery.md, fonts.md and review-hero.md, because those pages read as more "made" than ours in side-by-side review.
- **Premium scale** <!-- rule:premium -->: hero head 84-120px at 1440 (long head 64px), section heads 48-60px, item titles 24-28px, body 16-18px; display tracking -0.01 to -0.03em. One container 1312px on 12 columns, sections 120-176px apart, 6-9 sections, one idea per screen, dense followed by quiet.
- **Hero posture** <!-- rule:premium-hero -->: drawn by assign.py (Poster, Stage, Ledge, Corridor, Counter, Index). First screen = nav, head of 7 words or fewer, one lead sentence, one or two actions, the visual. No label above the head, no stat row, chips, filters or badges.
- **Premium type** <!-- rule:premium-type -->: display and body faces come from Hallmark's refined classes (some from Fontshare). Serif display and one italic phrase are allowed when the face has a real italic, never as cream + serif + rust.
- **Pictures** <!-- rule:premium-pictures -->: big or not at all (6+ of 12 columns and 440px+ tall, or full-bleed); one treatment on every photo (assign.py draws it); photos from `scripts/photos.py` (Hallmark's tagged library), same temperature and tone across the page; drawn pictures only as full-bleed key art for fictional worlds. Off-subject photos are cut, not kept to fill a slot.
- **Restraint** <!-- rule:premium-restraint -->: space over boxes, hairlines over borders, no card shadows, two button looks at most, one accent on under 5% of the page.
- **Real fonts in screenshots** <!-- rule:real-fonts-in-shots -->: every screenshot script calls `fontroute.attach(context)` (scripts/fontroute.py) before `goto`; check.py does it itself. Fallback fonts make every check judge the wrong page.
