# Three short examples (method, not looks) <!-- rule:short-examples -->

**Season site for a regional orchestra** · persuade.
Rut: a full-bleed concert photo with a "Buy tickets" button; its opposite, a black minimalist page with one serif word. Ranked candidates: #1 concert programme booklet, #2 conductor's score, #3 municipal poster wall, #4 ticket-office seat chart, #5 rehearsal call sheet. The script rolled #3. Contract thesis: "The poster wall serves 'find a concert and buy seats' because each concert is a two-colour poster tile, and the seat map uses the same two colours, darker = pricier." Hue from the hall's oxblood seats. One move: the wall of tiles.

**Expense tool for a small bakery** · operate.
Rut: grey dashboard with a donut chart; its opposite, a cute illustrated app. Ranked: #1 cash register receipt, #2 order spike, #3 flour-sack stencil type, #4 bakery chalkboard price list, #5 supplier delivery note. Rolled #4. The world lends type, palette and one move only: categories are chalkboard colour chips, layout stays a standard table and filter bar. Hue from the chalk colours on slate, light scene, committed rail.

**Interactive essay on river flooding** · experience.
Rut: a satellite photo hero with scroll-triggered charts; its opposite, a hand-drawn watercolour story. Ranked: #1 water-level gauge, #2 flood map, #3 sandbag wall, #4 parish flood marks on a church wall, #5 hydrologist's field form. Rolled #4: each chapter is a carved high-water mark, and the scroll position is the water rising past them. A plain, still reading mode is one click away.
